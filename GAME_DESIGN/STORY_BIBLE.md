# Story Bible — PROJECT: SHADOW FRONT

## Working premise
In the fictional region of Veyra, a chain of coordinated blackouts and fabricated emergency broadcasts pushes neighbouring communities toward conflict. A small multinational crisis-response unit is assigned to locate missing relief personnel and establish what happened. The investigation reveals that several groups are being manipulated by a hidden information network.

The story is about trust, accountability, civilian protection, and the cost of decisions made under pressure. The central mystery should unfold through character relationships, evidence, conflicting accounts, and consequences—not through exposition dumps.

## Originality rules
- All nations, cities, organizations, characters, insignia, dialogue, and events must be original.
- Do not recreate real conflicts or identifiable real-world units.
- Do not copy story beats, named characters, mission layouts, or dialogue from existing franchises.

## Core cast — provisional
- **Mara Venn** — field-team lead; calm under pressure, privately doubts the intelligence that began the operation.
- **Elias Rook** — communications and systems specialist; observant, dry humour, values evidence over assumptions.
- **Nadia Sayegh** — medic and humanitarian liaison; keeps the team focused on people affected by the crisis.
- **Tomas Ilyan** — reconnaissance and navigation specialist; loyal to the team, uneasy about the mission's changing objectives.
- **Player character: Alex Mercer (placeholder)** — customizable callsign and appearance where feasible; a capable new member whose perspective anchors the story.

Names and roles are placeholders and should be reviewed before final production.

## Narrative structure — provisional 10-mission campaign
1. **Cold Signal** — opening incident; introduce the team, controls, and mystery.
2. **The Quiet District** — search for missing relief staff in a city under blackout.
3. **Paper Trails** — investigate contradictory records and meet a key witness.
4. **Long Way Home** — escort survivors through a changing environment.
5. **False Flag** — discover evidence that challenges the team's original briefing.
6. **Broken Relay** — restore a communications link and reconnect separated characters.
7. **The Witness** — protect a source while deciding what information can be trusted.
8. **No Clean Answers** — the team fractures over a difficult choice.
9. **The Last Broadcast** — expose the network behind the fabricated emergency.
10. **After the Noise** — finale, consequences, and character resolutions.

This is a planning outline, not a locked script. Each mission needs a detailed beat sheet, playable objective flow, dialogue plan, environment list, audio plan, and acceptance tests before implementation.

## Narrative quality rules
- Every mission must change the story or a character relationship.
- Give squad members distinct voices and useful emotional arcs.
- Use quiet scenes to balance action sequences.
- Let the player understand objectives without relying only on waypoint markers.
- Choices should have understandable consequences; avoid fake choices that do not matter.
