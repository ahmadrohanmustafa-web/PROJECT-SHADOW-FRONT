# Mission 02 — The Quiet District

## Narrative purpose
Move the story from the isolated Northstar Terminal to a lived-in district affected by the blackout. Introduce Mara Venn and Nadia Sayegh through decisions and conversation, widen the human cost of the fabricated alert, and establish that the VEYRA-7 relay is part of a larger information chain.

## Location and art direction
A compact fictional district called Larkspur Ward: a transit plaza, a small community clinic, a shuttered market street, and a municipal records office. The blackout is recent. Emergency lighting, battery lanterns, handwritten notices, and quiet public spaces should communicate disruption without relying on destruction or graphic imagery.

## Mission flow
1. **Arrival — The lights are still out**
   - A short arrival scene establishes the district and the squad's immediate goal.
   - Mara briefs the team: locate two missing relief coordinators last seen near the clinic.
   - Player control begins after the briefing.
2. **Clinic reception**
   - Meet Nadia Sayegh, who is helping residents and has kept a paper intake list.
   - The player checks a simple set of non-sensitive records with Nadia's permission.
   - The list shows the coordinators were redirected to the municipal records office before the public alert was issued.
3. **Market passage**
   - Traverse a quiet, partially lit street.
   - Optional environmental stories: a closed shop notice, a community charging station, and a volunteer message board.
   - Elias compares the time on a local notice with the Northstar terminal record.
4. **Records office**
   - Restore a local terminal using a fictional, abstract interaction puzzle.
   - Recover a dispatch receipt referencing the same VEYRA-7 relay.
   - The receipt proves the redirect was entered under an unfamiliar administrative account; it does not identify the person behind it.
5. **Reunification**
   - Find the coordinators safe in a public shelter room.
   - Nadia confirms they were assisting residents and were never missing.
   - The player chooses whether to share the recovered discrepancy immediately with Mara or first ask Nadia to verify the paper record.
6. **Debrief — A pattern, not an answer**
   - The squad compares the two records.
   - Mara orders a cautious follow-up rather than making an accusation.
   - A final message indicates that another district received a similar false alert.

## Character beats
- **Mara Venn:** measured leadership; she distinguishes verified facts from assumptions.
- **Nadia Sayegh:** practical compassion; she protects residents' privacy and challenges the team to remember who is affected by the investigation.
- **Elias Rook:** links the Northstar timestamp to the district receipt, but refuses to guess at the sender.
- **Player character:** earns the team's trust by listening, checking evidence, and communicating clearly.

## Gameplay systems introduced
- A small hub-like district with readable landmarks.
- Optional environmental story objects.
- A simple evidence review interaction.
- A low-pressure choice that changes the debrief dialogue, not the core mission outcome.
- Companion dialogue that responds to mission phase.
- Save points at the clinic and records office.

## Production boundaries
- Keep the district compact and modular for the Intel HD 520 target.
- Use static geometry and baked/static lighting where practical.
- No graphic depictions of civilian harm.
- No real-world operational procedures, weapons instruction, or transferable tactical guidance.
- Use original signage, architecture, text, audio, and visual assets.

## Acceptance tests
- The mission can be completed without collecting optional environmental notes.
- Optional notes never block progression.
- Both dialogue choice branches converge on the same verified evidence state.
- The coordinators are clearly established as safe.
- The VEYRA-7 connection advances the mystery without revealing the hidden network.
- All mission-critical information is available in subtitles/text, not audio alone.
- Checkpoints restore the correct mission phase after restarting.
