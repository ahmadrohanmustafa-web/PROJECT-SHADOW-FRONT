# Mission 03 — Paper Trails

## Narrative purpose
Turn the Larkspur discovery into a verifiable chain of evidence. The squad compares an administrative ledger with an earlier paper copy and records a witness account without accusing an unknown person.

## Location
A compact records annex and public-records office in Larkspur's third district, with a community witness pavilion and a small notice board. The space uses static, low-detail geometry for the Intel HD 520 target.

## Playable flow
1. Reach the records annex.
2. Speak with archivist Imani, who explains that an earlier copy was retained.
3. Compare the amended dispatch ledger at the public-records terminal.
4. Collect a witness statement confirming the paper copy was sealed earlier.
5. Return to the plaza briefing point for debrief.

## Optional story
The community notice board records a local blackout timeline. It adds context but never blocks mission completion.

## Character and evidence beats
- Imani distinguishes an administrative amendment from proof of who requested it.
- Elias compares timestamps and refuses to infer an identity from incomplete records.
- The witness confirms the sealed paper copy predates the digital amendment.
- The squad leaves with a corroborated discrepancy, not an unsupported accusation.

## Production boundaries
Original fictional setting and dialogue. No graphic content, real-world operational procedures, or transferable tactical instruction. All geometry is static and uses no dynamic shadows.

## Acceptance tests
- Mission loads independently from the campaign menu.
- Five objectives progress in order.
- The optional notice board never blocks progression.
- Objective progress and completion persist in the shared campaign save.
- Mission 03 unlocks only after Mission 02 completion.
- Returning to the campaign menu and using Continue resumes the latest incomplete mission.
