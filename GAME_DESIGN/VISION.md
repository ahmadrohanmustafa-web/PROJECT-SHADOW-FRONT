# PROJECT: SHADOW FRONT — Game Vision

## Product
An original, single-player, first-person cinematic action campaign for Windows. The finished product is intended to be a straightforward download and play experience; players should not need to use a game editor or write code.

## Target hardware and performance
Primary target: HP ProBook 450 G3 class machine with Intel Core i5-6200U, Intel HD Graphics 520, 16 GB RAM, and SSD. Initial baseline is 1280x720 with a performance-first low preset. Actual performance must be measured on the target machine before making frame-rate promises.

## Storage
Aim for approximately 15 GB, with an absolute 20 GB download/install ceiling. Quality and variety matter more than filling the budget. Use efficient asset production and compression.

## Engine direction
Godot 4.x with the Compatibility renderer, targeting a standalone Windows build. Exact stable version will be pinned before implementation.

## Creative direction
- Tone: grounded, tense, human, and cinematic; serious without glorifying violence.
- Setting: a fictional present-day world with invented countries, agencies, factions, locations, and political history.
- Identity: inspired by the pacing and production ambition of premium military story campaigns, while remaining an original IP. No copied characters, missions, dialogue, maps, logos, music, or distinctive assets.
- Player fantasy: be part of a close-knit team whose relationships and choices matter as much as mission outcomes.

## Experience goals
1. A coherent campaign with a beginning, escalation, turning point, climax, and meaningful ending.
2. Responsive first-person controls with configurable key bindings, sensitivity, accessibility options, and controller support where practical.
3. Convincing movement animation and camera feedback without making controls feel sluggish.
4. Squad companions with distinct personalities, contextual dialogue, and believable reactions.
5. Distinct mission rhythms: investigation, traversal, rescue, tense infiltration, defensive set pieces, and story-focused sequences.
6. Fictional equipment with distinct visual identity, sound, handling feel, and balanced gameplay roles.
7. Layered audio: ambience, weather, footsteps, dialogue, music, UI feedback, and spatialized world sounds.
8. Strong performance, stability, and memory budgets for the target laptop.

## Visual strategy
Prioritize believable art direction over expensive photorealism: strong composition, detailed but efficient environments, careful lighting, animation, atmospheric audio, and consistent materials. Avoid dependence on ray tracing or costly high-end rendering features. Offer scalable settings.

## Safety and originality boundary
All conflict, equipment, and action systems are fictional entertainment abstractions. Do not implement real-world weapon training, operational tactics, or instructions that could transfer to real-world violence. Keep gameplay non-instructional and non-graphic.

## Definition of done
A feature is not done merely because code exists. Track it as Designed, Implemented, Automated-tested, Playtested, and Polished. A release is not ready until the exported Windows build has been tested on the target hardware.
