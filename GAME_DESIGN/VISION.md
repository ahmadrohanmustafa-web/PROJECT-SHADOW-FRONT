# PROJECT: SHADOW FRONT — Game Vision

## Product
An original, single-player, first-person modern military action campaign focused on character-led storytelling, cinematic pacing, readable combat encounters, companion teamwork, responsive controls, and a memorable audio-visual identity.

## Creative direction
- Tone: grounded, tense, human, and cinematic; serious without glorifying violence.
- Setting: a fictional present-day world with invented countries, agencies, factions, locations, and political history.
- Identity: inspired by the pacing and production ambition of premium military story campaigns, while remaining an original IP. No copied characters, missions, dialogue, maps, logos, music, or distinctive assets.
- Player fantasy: be part of a close-knit team whose relationships and choices matter as much as mission outcomes.

## Experience goals
1. A coherent campaign with a beginning, escalation, turning point, climax, and meaningful ending.
2. Responsive first-person controls with configurable key bindings, sensitivity, accessibility options, and controller support where the engine permits.
3. Convincing movement animation and camera feedback without making controls feel sluggish.
4. Squad companions with clear personalities, contextual dialogue, readable follow/fallback/support behaviours, and believable reactions.
5. Distinct mission rhythms: investigation, traversal, rescue, tense infiltration, defensive set pieces, and story-focused sequences.
6. Fictional equipment with distinct visual identity, sound, handling feel, upgrade paths, and balanced gameplay roles.
7. Layered audio: ambience, weather, footsteps, dialogue, music, UI feedback, and spatialized world sounds.
8. Strong performance targets and repeatable tests on the selected hardware.

## Scope philosophy
Build a polished vertical slice first, then expand using reusable systems. A complete campaign is the destination; it is not considered complete until every mission is playable, tested, and connected to the story.

## Safety and originality boundary
All conflict, equipment, and action systems are fictional entertainment abstractions. Do not implement real-world weapon training, operational tactics, or instructions that could transfer to real-world violence. Keep gameplay readable and non-instructional.

## Definition of done
A feature is not "done" merely because code exists. Track it as:
- Designed
- Implemented
- Automated-tested
- Playtested
- Polished

Only mark a stage after evidence exists.
